# Research Standards & Methodology

For every research task, adhere to the following professional methodology:

## 1. Core Directives
* **Evidence-Based:** Always ground findings in reliable, primary sources.
* **Cross-Verification:** Never rely on a single source of truth. Cross-reference findings across multiple documents, APIs, or files.
* **Alternative Viewpoints:** Actively search for and document alternative or conflicting viewpoints and methodologies.
* **Confidence Estimation:** Clearly state the confidence level of your findings (High, Medium, Low) and list any gaps in information.

## 2. Process Workflow
1. **Identify Uncertainty:** Clearly isolate what needs to be researched.
2. **Formulate Search Queries:** Write targeted query strings and execute focused searches.
3. **Analyze & Fact-Check:** Separate verified facts from inferences, opinions, or assumptions.
4. **Synthesize Findings:** Outline conflicting details, state remaining uncertainties, and present clear, evidence-backed conclusions.
